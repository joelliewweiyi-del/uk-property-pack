## 1. Summary and recommendation

Recommendation: do not proceed at the asking price. Re-bid at no more than GBP 2,082,000 (rounded down from GBP 2,082,101, the OM's own 5.32% yield applied to the proven rent), subject to the conditions in section 9.

- The OM asks GBP 2,150,000 (om-22-canal-street.pdf p.1 "Offers are invited for the freehold at GBP 2,150,000, subject to contract.") and claims a 5.32% NIY (om-22-canal-street.pdf p.1 "This reflects a net initial yield of 5.32% after purchaser costs of 6.8%.") on GBP 122,200 (om-22-canal-street.pdf p.2 "Total contracted rent: GBP 122,200").
- The leases and the vendor's own rent roll prove GBP 118,300 (rent-roll-22-canal-street.pdf p.1 "Total passing rent: GBP 118,300"). The GBP 3,900 a year gap is all on unit 4B: OM GBP 52,000 (om-22-canal-street.pdf p.2 "4B Copperleaf Coffee Ltd Commercial GBP 52,000 31 March 2031") vs lease GBP 48,100 (lease-canal-4b.pdf p.2 "The annual rent is GBP 48,100, payable quarterly in advance.").
- NIY on the proven passing rent: 5.15%, i.e. 0.17 percentage points below the OM's 5.32%.
- Other material points: a tenant break on 4B in 2029 that the OM denies; the 5C tenant has given notice (GBP 25,200 a year at risk); GBP 3,900 arrears on 2A; an expired gas safety record and an expired EICR on 2A and an open C2 electrical defect on 5C, all contrary to the OM's "All gas safety and electrical certificates are current."

The OM's GBP 52,000 is not reachable even at the 2027 review: GBP 52,000 / GBP 48,100 = an 8.1% uplift, against a 4% cap.

## 5. Price and yield

- Asking price: GBP 2,150,000 (om-22-canal-street.pdf p.1 "Offers are invited for the freehold at GBP 2,150,000, subject to contract.")
- Purchaser costs at 6.8% (om-22-canal-street.pdf p.1 "after purchaser costs of 6.8%"): GBP 146,200.
- Price plus purchaser costs: GBP 2,296,200
- NIY on the proven passing rent: 5.15% (GBP 118,300 / GBP 2,296,200).
- The OM's 5.32% is its own arithmetic on the wrong rent: GBP 122,200 / GBP 2,296,200 = 5.32%. The method is right, the rent is not.

Price effect of the 4B rent error: value at the OM's 5.32% NIY on GBP 118,300 = GBP 118,300 / 0.0532 / 1.068 = GBP 2,082,101, which is GBP 67,899 (3.2%) below the asking price. (Using the unrounded OM yield, GBP 2,150,000 x 118,300 / 122,200 = GBP 2,081,383.)

Sensitivity to 5C leaving and not re-letting: Let income without flat 5C: GBP 93,100 (48,100 + 21,600 + 23,400), giving 4.05% on GBP 2,296,200.

The PM report is internally inconsistent: pm-report-22-canal-street-q3-2026.pdf p.1 "Rent collected in the quarter: GBP 27,625, which is 100% of rent demanded." against "Rent demanded in the quarter: GBP 29,575". GBP 27,625 / GBP 29,575 = 93.41%, not 100%; the GBP 1,950 shortfall is the 2A arrears it reports itself ("Arrears at 30 September 2026: GBP 1,950 (flat 2A, September rent)."). Arrears have doubled in one month. November rent fell due on 1 November and the rent roll does not say whether it was paid, so arrears may already be GBP 5,850. The 2A tenant also reports a heating fault (mail m12 "there has been no hot water since Saturday"), which may be a reason for withholding rent.

Price effect: arrears belong to the vendor; require that they be excluded from the price and not assigned, or deducted at completion (GBP 3,900 now).
